# CommuniServe SA — Privacy Policy
_Last updated: 1 November 2026_

CommuniServe SA ("the app") is a student project that helps people find local services and events.

**What we collect:** your name and email (to create your account or sign in with Google); reviews and listings you submit; an optional location when you tap “Use My Location”; and a device notification token so we can send alerts.

**How we use it:** to run your account, show your reviews/listings, and deliver notifications you have allowed. We do **not** sell data or show ads, and we do not share it with third parties other than the services that run the app (Google Firebase for sign-in and notifications, and our API host).

**Security:** passwords are stored only as bcrypt hashes; data is sent over HTTPS.

**Your choices:** switch notifications off in Settings; deny location permission; sign out at any time; ask us to delete your data at the contact address below.

**Children:** the app is not directed at children under 13.
